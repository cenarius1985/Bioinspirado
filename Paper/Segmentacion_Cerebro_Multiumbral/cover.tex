I am pleased to submit our manuscript titled ``Enhancing Brain Image Segmentation: A Metaheuristic Approach to Multi-Threshold Optimization'' for consideration for publication in Natural Computing. Our research presents a comprehensive study of brain image segmentation using eight metaheuristic optimization algorithms, including the Grey Wolf Optimizer and Tuna Swarm Optimization. This work is particularly relevant to the field of medical image processing, as it addresses the critical need for precise segmentation techniques in diagnosing and treating neurological disorders.

The study’s findings demonstrate that the Grey Wolf Optimizer consistently outperforms other algorithms across key metrics such as the Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio and Structural Similarity Index, making it a promising tool for clinical and research applications. This paper offers significant contributions, especially in advancing automated methods for diagnosing brain diseases through enhanced segmentation accuracy.

We confirm that this manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to Natural Computing. We have adhered to all ethical guidelines as required by your journal.

Thank you for considering our manuscript. We look forward to your feedback and hope that our work will be accepted into Natural Computing.
